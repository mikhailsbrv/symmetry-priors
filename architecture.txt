LayerNorm(X):
  mu = mean(X, dim=-1, keepdim=True)           -> [B, 197, 1]
  var = mean((X-mu)^2, dim=-1, keepdim=True)   -> [B, 197, 1]
  (X-mu) / sqrt(var + 1e-6) * gamma + beta     -> [B, 197, 192]
  gamma, beta: [192] (different in each LayerNorm)
